Field Service Engineer I - Ulvac Technologies

Supported production semiconductor fabs by rebuilding critical hardware, modernizing subsystems, and reducing recurring failures through structured troubleshooting and retrofit execution.

Overview

High-volume fabs depend on precise, continuously available equipment. In this role I worked on advanced semiconductor tools where a single reliability issue could affect throughput, yield, and customer schedules. The work required rapid technical diagnosis, disciplined execution, and close coordination with both factory teams and internal engineering groups.

Implementation

I rebuilt and installed an upgraded 8-inch etch chuck assembly, integrated a new VAT system and circuit breaker, and modified valve-control software and wiring to support the new configuration. Beyond that single upgrade, I maintained tools across six fabs through preventive maintenance, calibration, and data-driven repairs, and I executed more than 30 retrofit projects spanning vacuum, RF, motion-handling, PLC, and HMI subsystems.

Results

The upgraded chuck assembly improved tool flexibility, increased wafer temperature uniformity, and reduced unplanned downtime by 15%. Across the broader fleet, the maintenance and retrofit work increased uptime by 18%, improved process stability by 22%, and extended tool life on systems with heavy production demand. Standardized troubleshooting procedures also reduced average fault diagnosis time by 35%.
