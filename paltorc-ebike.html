Mechanical Engineering Intern - Paltorc

Integrated connected mobility hardware and software for an E-Bike platform, combining telemetry, diagnostics, and customer-facing product development.

Overview

The E-Bike platform needed better linkage between on-bike hardware and the companion software experience so riders could access telemetry and the team could debug issues more effectively.

Implementation

I implemented end-to-end integration between the control module and companion app, capturing heart rate, temperature, weather, and incline in real time. I also diagnosed and fixed mechanical and software issues and developed the official E-Bike website and mobile application.

Results

The system delivered richer real-time data to riders and improved overall platform reliability by tightening the loop between sensing, software, and testing.
