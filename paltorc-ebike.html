Mechanical Engineering Intern - Paltorc

Connected an E-Bike control module to rider-facing software to bring live telemetry, diagnostics, and product integration into one system.

Focus Connected mobility and embedded product integration
Telemetry Heart rate, temperature, weather, incline
Scope Hardware, software, and rider-facing product support
Outcome Richer rider data and stronger reliability

Overview

The goal of this internship was to tighten the connection between the E-Bike hardware and the rider software experience. That meant not only exposing more useful live data, but also making the broader platform easier to debug and iterate on during development.

Implementation

I integrated the control module with the companion app so it could stream heart rate, temperature, weather, and incline data in real time. I also diagnosed and resolved mechanical and software issues that affected reliability, and contributed to the official E-Bike website and application.

Results

The result was a more connected product experience, with better visibility into ride conditions and a tighter loop between sensing, software, and product debugging. It gave users more context while improving the development team's ability to troubleshoot issues across the full stack.

What I Did

  • Integrated embedded hardware telemetry with the mobile application.
  • Streamed rider and environmental signals in real time.
  • Diagnosed mechanical and software issues affecting product reliability.
  • Contributed to both the official website and application experience.
