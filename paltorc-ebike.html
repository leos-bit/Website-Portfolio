Mechanical Engineering Intern - Paltorc

Integrated connected mobility hardware and software for an E-Bike platform, combining telemetry streaming, diagnostics, and customer-facing product development.

Overview

The E-Bike platform needed a tighter connection between the vehicle control module and the rider software experience. The goal was to expose richer live data to users while also making the system easier to diagnose and maintain during product development.

Implementation

I implemented end-to-end integration between the control module and companion app, capturing heart rate, temperature, weather, and incline data in real time. I also diagnosed mechanical and software issues that affected reliability and contributed to the official E-Bike website and mobile application.

Results

The resulting system gave riders better live visibility into ride conditions and created a more integrated product experience. It also improved platform reliability by tightening the loop between sensing, software, and issue resolution during development.
